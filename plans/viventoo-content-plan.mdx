Viventoo publishes one article every Tuesday from 17 November 2026 to 30 March 2027. Eight of the 17 drafts in Ghost become articles, the other nine are merged, held or deleted, and twelve new or updated pieces fill the calendar. This page gives the calendar, the decision for each draft, the repairs to published posts and the changes the registration sites need.

<Info>
  Summary of "Viventoo – plán obsahu (nov 2026 – mar 2027)" in the OneDrive Plan folder (03 Obsah Viventoo), dated 15 November 2026. The OneDrive file is the master copy and holds the full evidence, sources and limits of the data.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Organic search will not carry this winter. In the three months to 12 November 2026, Search Console recorded 26 clicks for programovaniepredeti.sk, 35 for partydeti.sk, 4 for taborypredeti.sk and 0 for viventoo.com. Every article is scheduled for the campaign it feeds. Success is counted in vouchers, Open Day bookings and registrations on the registration sites.
</Warning>

## CTA codes

Every article ends with one CTA to a registration site. The link pattern is:

```text
?utm_source=viventoo&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=snippet_<x>&utm_content=<slug>
```

| Code | Landing page | `utm_campaign` | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PPD-demo | programovaniepredeti.sk, `#kontakt` | `snippet_ukazkova` | Live |
| PPD-krúžky | programovaniepredeti.sk/kruzky-pre-deti-trnava | `snippet_kruzky` | Page live, campaign name proposed |
| PPD-poukaz | programovaniepredeti.sk/darcekovy-poukaz | `snippet_poukaz` | Page missing, name proposed |
| PPD-školy | programovaniepredeti.sk/pre-skoly-a-firmy | `snippet_skoly` | Page missing, name proposed |
| TAB | taborypredeti.sk or a category page | `snippet_tabory` | Live |
| PARTY | www.partydeti.sk or a theme page | `snippet_oslavy` | Live |

The proposed campaign names wait for the owner's approval. See question 5 on [Decisions](/plans/decisions).

### Bundle for each article

Each article is followed by a social bundle: a Facebook and Instagram post (carousel or Reel), a Threads post and a YouTube Short. Articles tied to a sales campaign also get an ad variant and an e-mail. Parent-help topics such as Brainrot and Skibidi get an engagement boost only, with no sales ad. The bundle for each row is in the OneDrive master file.
