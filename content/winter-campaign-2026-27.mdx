## Status

Plan dated 13 November 2026, reviewed on 15 November 2026. The full plan is in the claude.ai project **SEO - Logiscool** as `claude/winter-campaign-plan-2026-27.md`, with a Slovak version and a 15-slide deck. This page is a summary and does not replace the plan.

## Summary

The campaign runs for 11 weeks as three overlapping tracks. Paid ads, e-mail and local partners carry the result this season, because organic search is still too small.

| Campaign | Dates | Main offer | Primary conversion | Lead site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Darček, ktorý má zmysel | 16 Nov – 23 Dec | Darčekový poukaz on a Logiscool program, with a gift pack | Voucher sold | programovaniepredeti.sk |
| Open Days and 2nd semester | 14 Dec – 31 Jan | Free ukážková hodina, then a beginner course from February | Open Day booking, then registration | programovaniepredeti.sk |
| Current families | 16 Nov – 31 Jan | Re-enrolment, Bring Your Buddy week, parent referral | Re-registration, referred booking | E-mail, MyLogiscool, classroom |
| Background: what Logiscool is | Whole period | Awareness content | Reach, video views, remarketing pool | viventoo.com |

Evidence for the paid-first approach, from Search Console for 10 August – 10 November 2026: programovaniepredeti.sk had 21 impressions for "programovanie pre deti" (position 24.8) and 29 for "kurz programovania pre deti" (position 17.4). Viventoo had 7 impressions in total.

The plan sets no numeric targets because there is no baseline. The enrolment target is the number of February beginner groups multiplied by 8.

## Gates before ad spend

No ad money is spent until all three are true:

1. Lead tracking sends one `generate_lead` event per successful form (task T0.8 in the SEO brief).
2. The "Zápis 2026 otvorený" badge on programovaniepredeti.sk shows the February intake.
3. The voucher page and the Open Day booking path work on a phone.

## Review findings

The plan follows the project rules on domain intent, landing pages and tracking. These points need fixing:

| Issue | In the plan | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo lesson age groups | Freshsales and booking forms use 6–8, 9–11, 12–14, 15+ | Use the owner's two groups: classes 1–4 and 5–8 |
| Channels | YouTube and Threads are missing | Post each Reel as a YouTube Short; repurpose short posts to Threads |
| Canva templates | Only HQ manual kits are used | Map the partner's monthly Canva templates to dates |
| Monthly plan format | Week-by-week tables | Add Canva template, owner and KPI columns |
| Coupons | GIFT code and 50% referral workshop voucher only | Define sibling and referral codes with validity, target page and tracking after the owner confirms terms |
| English version | Missing the 18-Reel creative section | Present only in the Slovak version |
| Voucher redemption | Tied to the first instalment of the February beginner course | Show a clear validity period and allow a change of group on the voucher page |
| Holiday-date articles | Viventoo articles on jarné and polročné prázdniny | Keep informational; CTA to the taborypredeti.sk waitlist |

<Warning>
The timeline is tight. Setup week starts on 16 November and the voucher page must be live by 23 November. The voucher product, price, validity, last pickup day and the monthly ad budget must be decided on 16–17 November.
</Warning>

## Open decisions

- Start date of the 2nd semester and the beginner groups opening in February
- Open Day dates: at least 12 slots, weekdays and weekends
- Voucher product, price, validity, discount, pickup hours and last pickup day
- From the master partner: whether GIFT is active in Slovakia, which Slovak creatives exist, and whether the referral campaign is on
- Monthly budget for Meta Ads and Google Ads
- Marketing consent wording for e-mail and ad audience uploads
- Photo and video consent list for children
- Who films, who edits and who answers leads on 23 Dec – 3 Jan
- Ghost analytics on Viventoo, which reports 0 views for all posts
