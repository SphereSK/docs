## Summary

The next 11 weeks run as three overlapping campaigns: a gift voucher push until 23 December, Open Days and 2nd-semester enrolment from late December to the end of January, and a retention and referral track for current families throughout. Paid ads, e-mail and local outreach carry the result this season, because organic search is still too small to do it.

The evidence for that last point, from Search Console on 13 Nov 2026: in the last three months programovaniepredeti.sk got 21 impressions for "programovanie pre deti" (position 24.8) and 29 for "kurz programovania pre deti" (position 17.4). Viventoo got 7 impressions in total. SEO work continues, but it will pay off in spring, not by Christmas.

**How to set the targets.** I have no baseline for leads or sales, so the plan sets none. The honest way to set them is capacity: decide how many beginner groups start in February, and multiply by 8 (the manual's optimal group is 6–12 students, minimum 4). That number is the enrolment target; Open Day bookings and ad budget follow from it.

**Three things must be true before any ad money is spent** (week of 16 Nov):

1. Lead tracking works: one `generate_lead` event per successful form, as in task T0.8 of the SEO brief.
2. The stale "Zápis 2026 otvorený" badge on programovaniepredeti.sk is replaced with the February intake.
3. The voucher page and the Open Day booking path exist and have been tested on a phone.

### The voucher, as the HQ kit defines it

The HQ "Give the Gift of Knowledge" program is a gift pack plus a gift card for one Logiscool program. The buyer registers with promo code GIFT and pays the program or the first course instalment, then collects the pack at the school. HQ allows an optional discount and recommends 5% at most. The buyers named in the kit are parents and grandparents.

Recommended local setup, for you to confirm:

- **Lead product:** the first instalment of a beginner course starting in February. It ties the gift to the 2nd-semester intake instead of competing with it.
- **Why not a workshop voucher as the lead:** the HQ business calendar says workshops should not be advertised during a course campaign, because courses carry the higher lifetime value. Keep the mini workshop as the referral reward only.
- **Delivery:** printable PDF by e-mail for last-minute buyers, plus pickup of the physical gift pack at Paulínska 20 until 22 December.
- **Page content:** what the child gets, age groups, how the recipient redeems it, validity, pickup hours, FAQ. Show the price on the page if it is in the structured data, as the SEO brief requires.
- **Message:** "Darček, ktorý sa nerozbije ani neomrzí. Darujte dieťaťu polrok, v ktorom si naprogramuje vlastnú hru."

### Content pillars for Instagram, Facebook and Viventoo

1. **What Logiscool is.** A 30-second "čo je Logiscool" Reel, trainer introductions, a classroom walk-through, how a lesson runs.
2. **What children make.** Screen recordings of student projects with the child's own commentary as voice-over, without faces unless consent is on file.
3. **Parent concerns.** The manual lists four: too much screen time, too early to start, maths is required, AI will replace programming. One Reel and one carousel per concern.

5. **Parent help.** Brainrot, Skibidi, WhatsApp safety, kyberšikana. These earn reach and shares; they sell nothing directly.

Test audiences first with one proven creative, then test creatives on the winning audience. Changing both at once tells you nothing.

| Excluded | Current course families | All acquisition ads | They get the referral and re-enrolment e-mails instead |

### Test sequence

| When | Test | Variants | Decide by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 23 Nov – 6 Dec | Voucher audience | A vs B vs C, same Reel and same static | Cost per voucher-page lead |
| 7 – 20 Dec | Voucher creative, on the winner | Gift angle ("darček, ktorý má zmysel") vs child-result angle (a student's game) vs unboxing of the gift pack | Cost per lead, then vouchers sold |
| 14 Dec – 3 Jan | Open Day awareness | Four concern Reels (screen time, too early, maths, AI) to A and D | Cost per 50% video view; builds audience E |
| 4 – 17 Jan | Open Day audience | A vs D vs F, with E always on | Cost per Open Day booking |
| 11 – 31 Jan | Open Day creative and form | Reel vs carousel of dates; website form vs Meta instant form | Cost per booking and show-up rate |

**Rules for every test.** One variable at a time. Equal daily budget per variant. Run each at least 7 days. Stop a variant early only if it has spent three times the average cost per lead without a lead. Judge on leads and bookings in Freshsales, not on clicks. Instant forms usually bring cheaper but weaker leads, so compare show-up rate, not form count.

### How to run it

1. Build one list in Freshsales this week: every primary school within reach, the library, and 30 employers with the most parents on staff. Record a named contact for each.
2. Send one short e-mail per group, written for that reader. For principals, lead with the free presentation, not with the courses.
3. Give every partner its own link with a UTM tag (for example `utm_source=zs-nazov&utm_medium=partner`) so bookings can be traced back.
4. Send partners a ready-to-paste newsletter paragraph and one image. If they have to write it, it will not go out.
