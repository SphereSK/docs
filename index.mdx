This site holds the marketing and SEO plans for Logiscool Trnava, a coding school for children and teens. It is the shared reference for everyone who works on the campaigns: the current campaign plan, the Viventoo editorial calendar, the owner's decisions and the working rules for content.

## The websites

Each domain owns one search intent. Content on one site must not compete with another.

| Domain | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| programovaniepredeti.sk | Year-round courses, the free demo lesson and enrolment |
| taborypredeti.sk | Summer camps |
| partydeti.sk | Birthday parties |
| viventoo.com | Articles for parents. It never sells directly. Every article ends with a button to one of the three sites above. |
| logiscool.sk | The official franchise page. We link to it but do not edit or copy it. |

## Rules that apply to all content

- Final content is in Slovak with correct diacritics, written for parents of children aged 6 to 18.
- Prices, dates, ages and offers come only from [Decisions](/plans/decisions) and the live registration sites. If a fact is missing, ask the owner.
- Every link from Viventoo, social posts, e-mails and partners carries UTM tags.
- Results are measured in vouchers sold, Open Day bookings, registrations and leads.
